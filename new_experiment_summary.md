  - Legacy Real-POCQi: Four models answered 125 single-turn clinical questions and evaluated all pairwise model combinations as blinded judges.
  - Legacy ChatDoctor: Four physician models generated multi-turn conversations for 125 patient scenarios, evaluated at 2, 4, 6, and 8 turns.
  - Patient-simulator pilots: Compared GPT- and Mistral-based patient simulators on 50 ChatDoctor cases, both with and without reference grounding.
  - MedSP1000 generation pilots: Tested the role-separated clinician–patient simulation pipeline on individual scenarios.
  - Expanded Real-POCQi generation: Eight models answered all 620 Real-POCQi questions.
  - Expanded Real-POCQi rubric and ranking: The same eight models judged all eight responses per question using a five-axis rubric and explicit ranking.
  - Qwen judge extension: Added two local Qwen models as judges to the expanded Real-POCQi experiment.
  - Real-POCQi direct ranking: Eight judges ranked responses without seeing an explicit rubric on a fixed 100-question subset.
  - Real-POCQi identity-revealed experiment: Six judges evaluated named model responses on a fixed 200-question subset, paired with the corresponding blinded condition.
  - Real-POCQi specialty analysis: Examined the completed blinded judgments across 30 clinical specialties.
  - Expanded MedSP1000 generation: Six clinician models completed four-exchange conversations for 200 standardized-patient scenarios using a fixed Mistral patient simulator.
  - MedSP1000 rubric and ranking: Six models judged the conversations independently at 2, 4, 6, and 8 visible turns.
