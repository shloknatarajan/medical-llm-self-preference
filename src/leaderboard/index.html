Study design

How to read this benchmark

Evaluation setup

Eight models answered all 620 Real-POCQi questions; six models completed 200 MedSP1000 scenarios. Judges scored and ranked blinded, randomly ordered answers from every model in the relevant cohort.

Primary measure

For the same answer to the same question, we compare its rank from the generating model with its mean rank from all outside judges. Negative differences indicate matched same-model affinity.

Adjustment

Results remove categorical presentation-position effects. Confidence intervals treat questions as independent units and average correlated judge effects within each question for pooled estimates.

Interpretation

The study measures affinity for realized answers, not conscious self-recognition. Style, reasoning conventions, response length, and family-specific quality criteria may mediate the observed effects.
